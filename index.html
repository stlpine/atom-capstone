A Real2Sim Pipeline for Humanoid Imitation Learning via Gaussian Splatting & XR Teleoperation

Collecting robot manipulation demonstrations inside a photorealistic 3DGS-reconstructed simulation using a VR headset, bridging the sim-to-real gap for humanoid learning.

Overview

Three Interconnected Objectives

We target an end-to-end pipeline: reconstruct a real environment, collect demonstrations via immersive VR teleoperation, and train a manipulation policy. Everything runs inside simulation.

Real-to-Sim Reconstruction

Reconstruct the real lab environment with 3D Gaussian Splatting and import it as a collision-aware physics scene in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, visually faithful and physically accurate.

XR Teleoperation

A Pico 4 Ultra VR headset streams hand and arm poses via WebXR. A dexterous retargeting module maps 21-keypoint hand skeletons to the Unitree G1 Dex3 fingers at 50 Hz over DDS.

Imitation Learning

Demonstrations are converted to a LeRobot-compatible dataset and used to train an ACT (Action Chunking with Transformers) policy, then evaluated in closed-loop inside the same sim.
